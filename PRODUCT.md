# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Restaurant guests arriving from Google who want to request a table quickly on mobile, plus outlet staff who review and confirm those requests in WalkinLeads.

## Product Purpose

Provide one direct reservation experience for Kampai, Basque, Embassy Vasant Kunj and Embassy Elan Epic, then route each request into the existing WalkinLeads workflow.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, atmospheric, assured. The restaurant photography leads; the interface feels like a refined hospitality invitation rather than a generic web form.

## Anti-references

Generic split-screen forms, pale SaaS cards, fake table maps, decorative gradients, and booking interfaces that imply a table is confirmed before staff approval.

## Design Principles

- Put the venue and its atmosphere first.
- Make the booking task obvious and fast on a phone.
- Use one shared interaction system with outlet-specific photography and colour.
- State confirmation status plainly at every step.
- Keep controls native, accessible and familiar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, touch targets of at least 44px, and reduced-motion support.
